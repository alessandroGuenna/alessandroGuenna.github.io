Energy Engineer

I turn data and energy systems
into concrete models and solutions.

I'm a young Energy Engineer from Italy, graduating in a few months, driven by a passion for innovative solutions that can lower our carbon footprint. The challenge ahead is immense. Global energy demand keeps rising year after year, and it cannot simply be dismissed: energy consumption is strongly correlated with human development and well-being. That's why I believe the answer is not to point fingers at developing countries for their CO₂ emissions, as is too often done. The real goal must be to decouple energy consumption from CO₂ emissions, so the world can keep developing without heating the planet. I'm convinced that technology is the way forward, and that's the contribution I want to make as an engineer.
